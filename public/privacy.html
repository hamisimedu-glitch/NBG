Who we are

Next Bridge Group Limited (“NBG”, “we”, “us” or “our”) is a real-estate development company based in Nyali, Mombasa, Kenya. This policy applies to the Ocean Mist Tower website.

Our published project address is Bandari Road, Nyali, Mombasa, Kenya. The contact details on the site will be updated when NBG publishes its phone number or email address.

Information and enquiry forms

The website’s enquiry forms request information such as your name, email address, phone number, apartment preference, viewing date and message. The online enquiry endpoint is not currently configured. Submitting a form does not send your details to NBG; the page tells you that the enquiry has not been sent. Information you enter remains in the form in your browser until you clear it or leave the page.

The site’s shortlist feature stores selected residence identifiers in local storage on your device so your shortlist can be shown again on a later visit. This shortlist is not sent to NBG. You can remove items using the shortlist controls or clear this site’s local storage in your browser settings.

When you load the site, your browser requests site resources and Google Fonts. The hosting provider and font service may receive technical request information such as your IP address and browser details to deliver those resources.

How information is used

Residence identifiers in your shortlist are used only to provide the shortlist feature in your browser. The form fields are not transmitted while the enquiry endpoint remains unconfigured. If NBG enables an enquiry endpoint, submitted details may be used to respond to you and manage your request; NBG should update this policy to identify the service and its retention practices before enabling that processing.

We do not currently use the website for advertising or analytics tracking.

Service providers and sharing

The site uses third-party hosting and Google Fonts. Those providers may process the technical information needed to serve their resources and apply their own privacy policies. We do not sell personal information.

If an enquiry service is connected in the future, information submitted through the form will be handled by the configured service; this policy should be updated with its details before that service is enabled. We may also disclose information where required by law.

Storage and retention

Shortlist selections remain in local storage on your device until removed or cleared. NBG does not receive enquiry form details through the current site configuration. Hosting providers may retain technical logs under their own practices. If you contact NBG through another channel, information is kept only as reasonably necessary to respond and meet legal requirements.

The website does not intentionally set advertising or analytics cookies.

Your privacy rights

Depending on applicable law, including Kenya’s Data Protection Act, 2019, you may have rights to request access to, correction or deletion of personal information, object to certain processing, or make a complaint to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (ODPC).

For a request about information shared with NBG outside this website, write to NBG at its published project address: Bandari Road, Nyali, Mombasa, Kenya. The online enquiry form is not connected and should not be used for privacy requests. You may also visit the ODPC website.

Security and children

We take reasonable steps to protect the website, but no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure. The website is intended for adults and is not designed to collect personal information from children.

Policy changes

We may update this policy when the website or our practices change. The latest version and its update date will be published on this page.

Questions about privacy?

Contact Next Bridge Group Limited at Bandari Road, Nyali, Mombasa, Kenya. Please do not use the unconnected online enquiry form for privacy requests.
