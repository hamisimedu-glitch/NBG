The development

An address that
redefines the everyday.

A tranquil haven in Nyali along Bandari Road, where luxury meets nature. Ocean Mist Tower pairs contemporary design with a touch of local architectural character to create a calm, sophisticated way of living.

From the screened balconies on its façade to the terrace crowning its roof, the tower is composed around light, air and the coastal horizon. Inside, open lounge and dining spaces, warm timber and soft layers invite you to slow down.
